# Roventhar Support

Need help with Roventhar?

Email **[ahmetdogukangurbuz@gmail.com](mailto:ahmetdogukangurbuz@gmail.com)** with the subject “Roventhar Support”.

To help us investigate, include your device model, iOS version, game version, what happened, and the steps that reproduce the issue. A screenshot is welcome, but please remove any personal information first. Never send passwords or payment details.

## About the game

Roventhar is a free single-player fantasy action game. No account is required. Move your mage with the joystick in the lower half of the screen and choose power cards between waves.

## Progress and saves

Progress is saved locally on your device. Avoid deleting the app while troubleshooting: deleting its data can erase your progress. The game does not provide its own cloud-save or account recovery service.

## If the game stops responding

Close and reopen the game, check that your device has available storage, and install available game updates. If the issue continues, email us with the details above.
