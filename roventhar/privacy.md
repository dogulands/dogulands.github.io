# Roventhar Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 9, 2026

This policy describes the current iOS release of **Roventhar**, provided by Ahmet Doğukan Gürbüz. For privacy questions, contact [ahmetdogukangurbuz@gmail.com](mailto:ahmetdogukangurbuz@gmail.com).

## Playing Roventhar

Roventhar is a free single-player game. You do not need to create an account or provide personal information to play. The current release does not include advertising, advertising identifiers, third-party analytics SDKs, or cross-app tracking. We do not sell or rent players' personal information.

## Game progress and settings

The game stores progress, equipment, in-game currency, skill progression, tutorial status, and preferences locally on your device so that you can continue playing. Roventhar does not upload these saves to a developer-operated server or provide its own cloud-save service.

Local data remains until it is removed from the device. Deleting the app and its data removes its local saves; merely offloading the app may keep them. Device backups may retain copies according to your Apple backup settings. We cannot access or remotely delete those backups or recover local saves that you have lost.

## Permissions

Roventhar does not ask to access your contacts, location, camera, microphone, or photo library to play the game.

## Support requests

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever information you choose to send, such as a description of a problem or screenshots. We use this information to respond and troubleshoot. Please do not send passwords, payment information, or other sensitive personal information.

Support correspondence is handled through our email provider and retained only as needed to resolve your request and meet applicable obligations. You may request deletion of correspondence by emailing us. We do not use support messages for advertising.

## Apple services

Apple operates the App Store, TestFlight, device backups, and platform diagnostic services. Depending on your settings and how you installed the game, Apple may process information and make crash reports, diagnostics, or TestFlight feedback available to the developer. We use any such reports to identify and fix problems. These services are governed by [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## This website

This policy and the support page are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical request information, such as IP addresses, when you visit. See [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement). We do not add analytics or advertising trackers to these Roventhar pages.

## Children and privacy

The game does not request personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information through support, please contact us so we can address the request and delete it where appropriate.

## Your choices and changes

You can remove local game data through your device settings and manage backups and diagnostic sharing through Apple settings. For questions or requests concerning information you sent us, email [ahmetdogukangurbuz@gmail.com](mailto:ahmetdogukangurbuz@gmail.com). Applicable law may provide additional privacy rights.

We will update this page if the game's privacy practices change and revise the date above.
