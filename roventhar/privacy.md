# Roventhar Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 13, 2026

This policy describes **Roventhar**, provided by Ahmet Doğukan Gürbüz. The optional services described below are included in version 1.0.1 and later; version 1.0 does not contain them. For privacy questions, contact [ahmetdogukangurbuz@gmail.com](mailto:ahmetdogukangurbuz@gmail.com).

## Playing Roventhar

Roventhar is a free single-player game. You do not need an account or to enable optional ads, analytics, or crash reporting to play. We do not sell or rent players' personal information.

## Version 1.0.1: optional services

**Rewarded ads.** If you choose an eligible age group and turn on optional rewarded ads in Settings, Roventhar may request a video ad from Google AdMob after Google's consent flow allows it. Ads are optional; there are no banners or forced breaks. Players under 13, and players who have not selected an age group, cannot turn on ads. For ages 13–15 we apply child-directed ad treatment; for ages 16–17 we apply teen treatment. We cap ad content at the general-audience rating and disable publisher personalization and first-party ID. We do not request Apple's App Tracking Transparency permission or intentionally access the advertising identifier. Ad availability may vary by region and consent choice.

When an ad is requested, Google and its advertising partners may process an IP address (which can indicate approximate location), device or app identifiers, ad views and interactions, and technical diagnostics for ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention, and service operation. The Google Mobile Ads SDK's [data disclosure](https://developers.google.com/admob/ios/privacy/data-disclosure) describes the data it may collect. Google's [privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) describes its handling of that data. Ad consent choices can be revisited in the game's Settings when available.

**Gameplay analytics.** Adults can separately opt in to Firebase Analytics. If enabled, we record limited game events such as a level starting or ending, equipment changes, and rewards earned, together with game values such as map, level, or reward amount. Firebase may generate an app-instance identifier for this installation and collect app and device information needed to operate Analytics. We do not send names, email addresses, birth dates, or saved-game files to Analytics. This choice is off by default.

**Crash reports.** Adults can separately opt in to Firebase Crashlytics. If enabled, crash reports may include stack traces, app state, device model, operating-system information, and diagnostics needed to identify and fix failures. This choice is off by default. Turning it off stops future collection and deletes unsent reports where supported, but cannot retract reports already delivered. Google describes Firebase's [App Store data practices](https://firebase.google.com/docs/ios/app-store-data-collection).

Your chosen age range and service switches are stored locally. We do not ask for a birth date or send the age range as a gameplay analytics event. Changing an ad or age choice after the ad SDK starts takes effect after restarting the game; ad requests stop immediately when ads are turned off.

## Game progress and settings

The game stores progress, equipment, in-game currency, skill progression, tutorial status, and preferences locally on your device so that you can continue playing. Roventhar does not upload these saves to a developer-operated server or provide its own cloud-save service.

Local data remains until it is removed from the device. Deleting the app and its data removes its local saves; merely offloading the app may keep them. Device backups may retain copies according to your Apple backup settings. We cannot access or remotely delete those backups or recover local saves that you have lost.

## Permissions

Roventhar does not ask to access your contacts, precise location, camera, microphone, or photo library to play the game. An approximate location may be inferred from an IP address during an optional ad request.

## Support requests

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever information you choose to send, such as a description of a problem or screenshots. We use this information to respond and troubleshoot. Please do not send passwords, payment information, or other sensitive personal information.

Support correspondence is handled through our email provider and retained only as needed to resolve your request and meet applicable obligations. You may request deletion of correspondence by emailing us. We do not use support messages for advertising.

## Apple services

Apple operates the App Store, TestFlight, device backups, and platform diagnostic services. Depending on your settings and how you installed the game, Apple may process information and make crash reports, diagnostics, or TestFlight feedback available to the developer. We use any such reports to identify and fix problems. These services are governed by [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## This website

This policy and the support page are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical request information, such as IP addresses, when you visit. See [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement). We do not add analytics or advertising trackers to these Roventhar pages.

## Children and privacy

The game does not request names, contact details, or birth dates from children. Players under 13 cannot enable in-game ads, gameplay analytics, or Firebase crash reporting. Eligible younger players may choose optional ads with age-appropriate settings. If you believe a child has sent us personal information through support, please contact us so we can address the request and delete it where appropriate.

## Your choices and changes

You can change optional service choices in the game's Settings, remove local game data through your device settings, and manage backups and Apple diagnostic sharing through Apple settings. To ask about information we or our providers may hold, or to request deletion where available, email [ahmetdogukangurbuz@gmail.com](mailto:ahmetdogukangurbuz@gmail.com). Because the game has no account, we may need details from you to locate a specific provider record. Applicable law may provide additional privacy rights.

We will update this page if the game's privacy practices change and revise the date above.
